Privacy Policy – Tank Riot: Arena Battle

Developer: LHSoft · Contact: lhsoft79@gmail.com · Effective date: 2026-11-18

Data stored on your device

Tank Riot saves your coins, unlocked tanks and maps, upgrades, tank colors, ammo, daily missions, records and settings (music, sound, vibration) locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. We do not operate our own servers, user accounts or analytics. The game does not ask for your name, email or any personal information.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform where required (for example in the EEA and UK). Google may collect and process data such as:

  • device identifiers (Android Advertising ID, where permitted),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics,

to deliver, measure and (with your consent) personalize ads. Data is encrypted in transit. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy. You can change your consent choice from the game's Settings (PRIVACY button, when shown) and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Vibration

The game uses the vibration of your phone for hit feedback. It can be turned off in Settings. This does not involve any personal data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
