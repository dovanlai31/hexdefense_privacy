Privacy Policy – Tank Riot: Mini Battle

Developer: LHSoft · Contact: lhsoft79@gmail.com · Effective date: 2026-11-21
App: Tank Riot: Mini Battle (Android, package com.tankriot.minibattle)

Tank Riot: Mini Battle is a game designed for children. We take their privacy seriously and keep data collection to a minimum.

We do not collect personal information

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no social features and no in-app purchases. It never asks for a name, email, photo, contacts or precise location. We do not operate our own servers or analytics.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (levels and stars, coins, tanks, upgrades, paints, stickers, daily missions) and settings (music, sound, screen shake, reduced flashes, graphics quality) are saved only on your device. This data is never sent to us.

Advertising

The game shows a small number of ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK):

  • Every ad request is marked as child-directed (COPPA "tag for child-directed treatment") and under the age of consent, with the maximum ad content rating set to G.
  • Ads are not personalized and there is no interest-based advertising or remarketing.
  • The game does not use the Android Advertising ID (the AD_ID permission is removed).
  • Video ads for bonus coins are optional and only play when the player taps a button.

To serve ads, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process limited technical information such as the device model, operating system version, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostics. Data is encrypted in transit. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The game uses internet access (to load ads) and vibration (for hit feedback, can be turned off in Settings). Neither involves personal data.

Children

The game complies with Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's personal information has been shared with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Chính sách quyền riêng tư (Tiếng Việt)

Tank Riot: Mini Battle không thu thập thông tin cá nhân: không tài khoản, không đăng nhập, không chat, không mua bán trong ứng dụng. Tiến trình chơi và cài đặt chỉ lưu trên máy và bị xóa khi gỡ game.

Game hiển thị một ít quảng cáo qua Google AdMob. Mọi yêu cầu quảng cáo đều được đánh dấu dành cho trẻ em, chỉ hiển thị nội dung mức G, không cá nhân hóa và không dùng mã quảng cáo (Advertising ID).
