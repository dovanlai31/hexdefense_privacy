Tiny Turbo Racing - Privacy Policy

Developer: LHSoft · Last updated: November 21, 2026

Tiny Turbo Racing ("the Game") is a racing game made for children and families. We care a lot about children's privacy. This policy explains, in plain words, what information is handled when the Game is played.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The Game has no accounts, no chat, no sign-in, no social features and no way to share photos, names or messages. We do not ask for a name, email, phone number or location.

Game progress (coins, stars, unlocked cars and settings) is saved only on the device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

2. Advertising

The Game is free and shows a small number of ads to support its development. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and are configured for a child-directed audience:

  • All ad requests are tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR), and limited to content rated G (general audiences).
  • Ads are not personalised and are not based on a child's behaviour or interests.
  • The Game does not access the device's Advertising ID.
  • Ads never appear while racing. A full-screen ad may appear occasionally between races, and an optional "watch a video for double coins" button is always clearly labelled and only plays when tapped.

To serve ads and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the device's IP address, device type, operating-system version and app interactions with ads. Google handles this information under its own policies, with child-directed protections applied: see Google Privacy Policy and AdMob and children's privacy.

3. Permissions

The Game only uses standard technical permissions needed to load ads (Internet access, network state and the background-task permissions used by Google Play services). It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

4. Children

The Game is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's personal information has been provided to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. In-app purchases and external links

The Game currently has no in-app purchases and no links that leave the Game.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
