Privacy Policy – Prism Swarm

Developer: LHSoft · Contact: lhsoft79@gmail.com · Effective date: 2026-11-15

Data stored on your device

Prism Swarm saves your crystals, workshop upgrades, unlocked ships, best survival time and settings (sound, screen shake, vibration) locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. We do not operate our own servers, user accounts or analytics.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform where required (for example in the EEA and UK). Google may collect and process data such as:

  • device identifiers (Android Advertising ID, where permitted),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics,

to deliver, measure and (with your consent) personalize ads. Data is encrypted in transit. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can change your consent choice from the game's Settings (Privacy options, when shown) and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes all locally stored data. You can also reset progress in Settings → Reset Progress.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
